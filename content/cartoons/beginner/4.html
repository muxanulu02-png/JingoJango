Cartoon Lesson 4: Daily Routines

Learn to talk about your daily activities in English

15
Words
3
Exercises
10 min
Duration

Vocabulary

  • Wake up Просыпаться
  • Brush teeth Чистить зубы
  • Have breakfast Завтракать
  • Go to school Идти в школу
  • Study Учиться
  • Have lunch Обедать
  • Play with friends Играть с друзьями
  • Do homework Делать уроки
  • Go to bed Ложиться спать

Exercises

1. What do you do first in the morning?
A) Go to bed
B) Wake up
C) Have dinner
D) Do homework
2. Complete: "I ______ my teeth after breakfast."
A) wake
B) brush
C) have
D) go
3. Put in correct order: get dressed → brush teeth → wake up
A) wake up → get dressed → brush teeth
B) wake up → brush teeth → get dressed
C) brush teeth → wake up → get dressed
D) get dressed → wake up → brush teeth
