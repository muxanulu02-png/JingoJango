Directions

1Watch the Video

Carefully watch the video lesson. Pay attention to the characters' pronunciation and gestures.

2Repeat the Phrases

Repeat the phrases out loud after the characters. Try to copy their pronunciation and intonation.

Listen and repeat:
"Hello, my name is Peppa"
3Learn Vocabulary

Go to the "Vocabulary" tab and study the new words and phrases from this lesson.

4Practice Speaking

Try to create your own dialogues using the phrases you've learned. Practice with a friend if possible.

Learning Tip:

Record yourself speaking and compare your pronunciation with the video. This will help you improve faster!

Vocabulary

Basic Greetings
Hello / Hi Привет
Good morning Доброе утро
Good afternoon Добрый день
Good evening Добрый вечер
Introduction Phrases
What's your name? Как тебя зовут?
My name is... Меня зовут...
Nice to meet you Приятно познакомиться
How are you? Как дела?

Questions

Practice these questions and answers:

Question 1:

What's your name?

Answer: My name is [Your Name]

Question 2:

How are you?

Answer: I'm fine, thank you! And you?

Grammar

Verb "to be" - Present Simple

I am (Я есть)

You are (Ты есть)

He/She/It is (Он/Она/Оно есть)

Sentence Building

Build a sentence:

Hello + my + name + is + Peppa

Hello, my name is Peppa!

Discussion

Discussion Topic:

How do you greet people in your country? What are the common greeting phrases?

Script

Peppa:

Hello! My name is Peppa. What's your name?

George:

Hi Peppa! My name is George. Nice to meet you!
