  4. Урок 3: Приветствия

Directions ▼

1 Watch the Video

Carefully watch the video lesson. Pay attention to how characters greet each other.

2 Repeat the Phrases

Repeat the greeting phrases out loud after the characters.

3 Learn Vocabulary

Study the new words and phrases in the Vocabulary section.

4 Practice

Complete all the activities to practice what you've learned.

Vocabulary ▼

  • Hello / Hi - Привет
  • Good morning - Доброе утро
  • Good afternoon - Добрый день
  • Good evening - Добрый вечер
  • What's your name? - Как тебя зовут?
  • My name is... - Меня зовут...
  • Nice to meet you - Приятно познакомиться
  • How are you? - Как дела?

Questions ▼

1. What is the most common greeting in English?

Answer: b) Hello/Hi

2. When do we say "Good morning"?

Answer: c) In the morning

3. How do you ask someone's name?

Answer: b) What's your name?

Grammar ▼

1. Choose the correct form of the verb "to be":

I Tom.

Answer: c) am

2. Complete the sentence:

Hello, my name Lisa.

Answer: a) is

Sentence Building ▼

1. Build a greeting sentence:

Hello / my / name / is / John

Answer: Hello, my name is John.

2. Build a question:

What / your / name / is / ?

Answer: What is your name?

Discussion ▼

1. How do people greet each other in your country?

2. What's the difference between formal and informal greetings?

3. When do you use "Hello" vs "Hi"?

Script ▼

Tom:

Hello! My name is Tom. What's your name?

Lisa:

Hi Tom! My name is Lisa. Nice to meet you!

Tom:

Nice to meet you too, Lisa! How are you?

Lisa:

I'm fine, thank you! And you?

Tom:

I'm great! Have a nice day!
